Receiver privacy

Receiver is a dash player. It does not require an account.

The queue, the last genre, and the lamp color stay on this device. Search queries are sent to YouTube so a song can be found. Video playback is handled by the YouTube player. Receiver does not sell personal information.

The Rec control uses the camera only after you tap it, to save a clip on this device. Receiver does not upload those clips.

Playback needs a network. The app face can open from the copy stored on the device.
